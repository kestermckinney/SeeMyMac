# SeeMyMac 0.1.3

First Linux release, distributed as a Flatpak (signed repository on GitLab Pages).

- Views and controls a Mac over Apple's High Performance Screen Sharing protocol, with plain VNC as a fallback.
- Syncs clipboard text, plays the Mac's audio, and copies files to and from the Mac.
- Passes Super and other system shortcuts to the Mac on Wayland.
